Our story

A space built on flexibility, not tradition

Emerald Grove started with a simple observation about Bangalore's event scene: plenty of banquet halls, plenty of conference rooms, and almost nothing built for everything in between.

Why we started

The gap between a hall and a hotel

Every month, Bangalore hosts thousands of workshops, baby showers, podcast tapings, engagement shoots, community meetups and founder offsites – none of which want a 500-seat banquet hall, and none of which fit comfortably in a beige conference room either.

Most venues in the city are built for one kind of event and priced accordingly. We built Emerald Grove for the opposite: one aesthetic, well-designed space in JP Nagar that adapts to whatever you're hosting, booked by the hour instead of forcing you into a full-day package you don't need.
