Common questions

Before you enquire

Is Emerald Grove good for a birthday party in Bangalore?

Yes – with seating for 50 and standing room for 100, Emerald Grove works well for birthday parties of most sizes, from an intimate gathering to a full celebration, with in-house or outside catering and décor either way.

Do you host corporate events and workshops?

Regularly. Corporate offsites, team workshops, hiring drives and product launches are some of our most common weekday bookings, with AV, projector and mic setup available on request.

How much does it cost to book the space?

Pricing is hourly-first, with half-day, full-day and extended-day options, and depends on your event type and day of the week. Enquire with your date and we'll send a quote – happy to talk through options.

Where is Emerald Grove located?

We're in JP Nagar, Bangalore – central and easy to reach, with street parking right outside.

Can I bring my own caterer or decorator?

Yes. Outside catering and décor are both welcome alongside our in-house options – just give us advance notice so we can plan around them.
