Policies & Guidelines

Last updated: 8 August 2026

These policies are a working draft and are pending final legal review before this venue accepts paid bookings. Numbers, notice periods and deposit amounts below are our proposed starting point, set out clearly so you know exactly what to expect – please treat them as provisional until a lawyer has signed off and this notice is removed.

01Booking & Payment Terms

Full payment confirms your slot

A booking is only confirmed once full payment of the quoted venue rental fee has been received. We don't hold dates on a partial payment, verbal confirmation, or "pencil booking" basis – until payment clears, the slot remains open to other enquiries.

Getting a quote

Because pricing depends on event type, duration and add-ons, we confirm your exact rate in writing (email or WhatsApp) before you pay. That written quote, once paid, becomes the agreed price for your booking.

How to pay

Bank transfer, UPI and [payment gateway/card – to be confirmed] are accepted. Payment instructions are shared along with your quote. A booking confirmation with your Booking ID is emailed once payment is received.

Taxes

Applicable taxes (including GST, once we are registered and required to charge it) will be added to invoices as per prevailing law and shown separately.

A separate, refundable security deposit may also apply depending on your event – see Security Deposit Policy below.
02Cancellation, Rescheduling & Refunds

How to cancel

Cancellation requests must be made in writing – email or WhatsApp, with your Booking ID – to be valid. Verbal or phone-call cancellations are not accepted, so there's a clear record for both sides.

Refund schedule

Refunds are calculated on the amount paid, based on how much notice you give before your event date:

Notice before eventRefund
15 days or more75%
7–14 days50%
3–6 days25%
Less than 72 hours, or no-showNo refund

Payment gateway or transfer charges, and any GST already remitted to the government, are non-refundable in all cases.

Rescheduling

One free reschedule is allowed if you request a new date at least 7 days before your original event date, subject to availability. Requests with less notice are treated as a cancellation under the schedule above, with a fresh booking made separately.

If we have to cancel

If Emerald Grove Events & Spaces cancels a confirmed booking, or circumstances beyond either party's reasonable control – natural disasters, government restrictions, public health emergencies and similar force majeure events – mean the event genuinely cannot go ahead, you'll receive a full refund or a free reschedule, whichever you prefer. We won't hold on to payment for a service we haven't provided.

Processing time

Approved refunds are processed within 7–10 business days to the original payment method.

03Security Deposit Policy

Most bookings – in-house or no catering, standard décor, no open flame – don't need a deposit. A refundable security deposit applies only where your event includes something with a real chance of causing damage or extra cleanup, so that everyday bookings aren't penalised for higher-risk ones.

When a deposit applies

Event includesRefundable deposit
Outside catering (not managed in-house)₹5,000
Décor involving nails, screws, strong adhesive, confetti or anything that can mark walls, floors or fixtures₹5,000
Large builds – stages, rigging, ceiling-mounted or heavy installations₹10,000
Open flame, sparklers, fire shows or pyrotechnics of any kind₹15,000

Where more than one of these applies to the same event, the highest single tier is the starting point – the final figure is confirmed in writing before the event once we know the full plan.

Fire and flame elements also require written approval at least 7 days in advance, a professional/certified operator only, a fire extinguisher on standby, and safe distance from drapes and soft furnishings. We reserve the right to stop any flame-based activity on the spot if it looks unsafe, deposit or no deposit.

Refunds

The deposit is collected ahead of the event and refunded in full within 5–7 business days afterward, minus a clear, itemised deduction for any damage, excess cleaning, or overtime beyond your booked slot.

04Venue Rules & Guest Conduct

Capacity

Guest numbers must stay within the confirmed capacity – 50 seated, 100 standing – for everyone's comfort and safety.

Check-in

The primary booking contact presents a valid government-issued photo ID on arrival and signs our check-in register, which records your Booking ID, name, mobile number, date, time in/out, guest count, ID type, and the last four digits only of your ID – we don't note or store the full number.

Alcohol & smoking

Neither is permitted on the premises except with our prior written approval. Where alcohol is approved, you're responsible for arranging any required excise/liquor permit for the day and for your guests drinking responsibly – we can refuse service or entry to anyone visibly intoxicated. Illegal substances are never permitted, no exceptions.

Sound & music

India's night-time noise rules mean amplified sound must stay contained indoors – doors and windows closed – after 10 PM; taking it outdoors past that hour needs prior permission from local authorities, arranged by you. Within the booked slot and indoors, play what you like.

Décor, vendors & outside help

Bring your own caterer or decorator, or use ours – either is fine with advance notice so we can plan around them. Vendors are responsible for their own equipment and cleanup unless agreed otherwise. See Security Deposit Policy for décor or catering that carries a damage risk.

Parking

Street parking is available right outside – please park considerately and avoid blocking neighbouring properties or driveways.

Children & pets

Children must be supervised by a guardian at all times. Pets are welcome with prior approval.

Timing

Please plan to vacate by the end time on your booking confirmation. Running over is charged at the applicable hourly extension rate (confirmed at enquiry) and depends on the next booking's availability.

Damage

Any damage to the venue, its fixtures, or rented equipment is charged to the client – deducted from the security deposit first where one applies, with anything beyond that billed separately.

We reserve the right to pause or end an event that breaches these rules, safety norms, or local law, without a refund.

06Privacy Policy

What we collect

Through our enquiry form: your name, phone number, email address and event details (date, guest count, event type, message). At check-in: your ID type and the last four digits of a government-issued photo ID – never the full number.

What we use it for

To respond to your enquiry, confirm and manage your booking, and communicate with you about your event. We don't use your details for unrelated marketing without asking first.

Where it's stored

Enquiry submissions are stored in a Google Sheet accessible only to Emerald Grove Events & Spaces, using Google's infrastructure (Sheets, Gmail) to process them on our behalf. Check-in register entries are kept securely on-site.

Sharing

We don't sell your data. It's only seen by team members who need it to manage your booking, or shared where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

For as long as needed to manage your booking plus reasonable record-keeping afterward (accounting, handling any dispute) – after which it can be deleted on request.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us to access, correct, or erase your personal data, or withdraw consent, by writing to us using the contact details below. We'll respond within a reasonable time.

This website

We don't use tracking cookies or analytics. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which involves your browser contacting Google's servers when you visit the site.
